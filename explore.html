Recommended places to travel in India

State Tourist places
Uttar Pradesh Taj Mahal (Agra)- A UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the Seven Wonders of the World. Built by Shah Jahan in white marble. India’s most visited tourist monument.

Varanasi- One of the oldest living cities in the world. Located on the banks of River Ganga. Major spiritual and pilgrimage center.

Ayodhya- Birthplace of Lord Rama. An important Hindu pilgrimage site. Known for temples and religious history.
Rajasthan Jaipur - The Pink City known for forts, palaces, and royal heritage.

Udaipur - The City of Lakes famous for palaces and scenic beauty.

Jaisalmer - The Golden City known for desert safaris and historic forts.
Goa Baga Beach - Popular beach known for nightlife and water sports.

Calangute Beach - The Queen of Beaches and a major tourist hub.

Basilica of Bom Jesus - UNESCO site famous for Portuguese architecture.
Kerala Munnar - Hill station with tea plantations and cool climate.

Alleppey - Famous for backwaters and houseboat tourism.

Kochi - Historic port city with strong colonial influence.
Himachal Pradesh Manali - Hill station famous for snow, adventure sports, and valleys.

Shimla - Former British summer capital with colonial architecture.

Dharamshala - Home of the Dalai Lama and center of Tibetan culture.
Maharashtra Mumbai - Financial capital of India and home of Bollywood.

Ajanta and Ellora - Ancient rock-cut caves and UNESCO World Heritage Sites.

Lonavala - Hill station known for waterfalls and greenery.
Tamil Nadu Chennai - Capital city famous for Marina Beach and culture.

Madurai - Temple city known for Meenakshi Amman Temple.

Ooty - Hill station famous for tea gardens and pleasant climate.
Karnataka Mysuru - Heritage city famous for Mysore Palace and Dasara festival.

Hampi - UNESCO site with ancient ruins and temples.

Bengaluru - IT capital known for technology parks and gardens.
Uttarakhand Rishikesh - Yoga Capital of the World located on River Ganga.

Haridwar - Holy city famous for Ganga Aarti and pilgrimages.

Nainital - Hill station famous for Naini Lake.
West Bengal Kolkata - Cultural capital known for literature, art, and history.

Darjeeling - Hill station famous for tea gardens and toy train.

Sundarbans - Largest mangrove forest and home of Royal Bengal Tiger.
Himachal Pradesh Manali – Famous for snowfall, adventure sports, and scenic valleys.

Shimla – Popular hill station with colonial charm and winter snow.

Spiti Valley – Cold desert region with monasteries and snow-covered mountains.
Jammu and Kashmir Gulmarg – One of India’s top snow and skiing destinations.

Pahalgam – Scenic valley with snowy landscapes and rivers.

Sonamarg – Meadow of gold known for glaciers and snowfall.
Sikkim Gangtok – Capital city with snow views and monasteries.

Tsomgo Lake – High-altitude lake that freezes in winter.

Nathula Pass – Snow-covered mountain pass on the Indo-China border.
Andgra Pradesh Tirupati- Famous for the Tirumala Venkateswara Temple. One of the richest and most visited temples in the world. A major pilgrimage site.

Visakhapatnam- A coastal city with beautiful beaches and hills. Known for RK Beach and submarine museum.

Araku Valley- A scenic hill station with coffee plantations. Popular for waterfalls and tribal culture.
